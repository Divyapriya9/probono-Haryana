How to get a free lawyer / मुफ़्त वकील कैसे मिलेगा

  1. Check if you qualify (below). / पात्रता देखें।
  2. Go to the District Legal Services Authority (DLSA) at the District Courts Complex, or the Sub-Divisional Legal Services Committee (SDLSC) at Tosham, Loharu or Siwani. Apply in writing or ask the staff. No fee.
  3. If you are in police custody or jail, or in court without a lawyer, tell the judge/magistrate or jail authority you want legal aid.
  4. The legal services authority assigns a panel lawyer to your case. In the legal-aid system, you normally do not pick the lawyer yourself.
  5. Legal aid is free for you: all panel lawyers are paid or supported by the authority, not by you. A lawyer who asks you for money for an assigned legal-aid case should be reported to the DLSA.
Who is eligible? (Section 12, Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987)
WomenChildrenMembers of SC / STPersons with disability Persons in custodyIndustrial workmenVictims of trafficking or begging Victims of mass disaster, caste atrocity, flood, drought, earthquake Persons whose income is below the limit set by the State Government
Summary only. The Act and State rules decide your case; the DLSA will tell you whether you qualify.
What does "Pro-Bono Basis" mean on the list below? The official panel list marks each advocate as Paid, Pro-Bono Basis or Ex-Servicemen. All of them serve legal-aid beneficiaries without charging the beneficiary. Pro-Bono Basis lawyers also do not take the authority's honorarium. This page shows only the Pro-Bono Basis advocates; the full panel (1,573 names) is in the official PDF.
